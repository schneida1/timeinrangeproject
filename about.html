The Interstitial Lag: Why Mentors Matter

One of the hardest things for newly diagnosed patients to understand is that Continuous Glucose Monitors (CGMs) do not measure blood directly; they measure interstitial fluid.

Because glucose takes time to travel from the bloodstream into the interstitial fluid, your CGM reading is actually 5 to 15 minutes "behind" your actual blood sugar. Endocrinologists explain the science, but peer mentors teach you how to pre-bolus your insulin to beat the lag.
