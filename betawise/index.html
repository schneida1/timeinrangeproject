The gap

CGMs made glucose data free.
Understanding it is still a privilege.

Most people see an endocrinologist for 15 minutes a few times a year. In between, 4,000 readings every two weeks pile up in an app that shows a squiggly line and an average. Betawise is the missing layer: it reads the data the way an expert would, every week, for everyone.

How it works

Two minutes to your first insight.

Bring your data

Connect Dexcom or drop in a Clarity or LibreView export. Analysis happens in your browser.

See what's really happening

Consensus metrics, your Glycemia Risk Index, an Ambulatory Glucose Profile, and patterns ranked safety-first.

Focus on one thing

Commit to a single weekly focus, make a plan with your coach, and bring a one-page report to your next visit.

What you get

Everything between appointments.

Clinical-grade metrics

Time in range, time in tight range, GMI, variability, and the Glycemia Risk Index — computed exactly as the International Consensus defines them.

Pattern engine

Detects overnight lows, dawn phenomenon, post-meal spikes, rebound highs, prolonged highs, and weekday/weekend differences.

One weekly focus

Safety first, then the biggest opportunity. We show how many points of time in range you could gain by fixing it.

AI peer coach

Talks like a friend who has lived with diabetes for years. Knows your patterns. Never gives dosing advice.

Clinic-ready report

A one-page summary in the format clinicians already use — AGP, consensus targets, daily profiles, and your questions.

Real human mentors

When software isn't enough, The Time in Range Project matches you with a trained peer mentor — free.

Built on the evidence

No black boxes where it matters.

Every number Betawise shows comes from a transparent, peer-reviewed definition. AI explains the numbers; it never invents them.

Privacy & safety

Designed so you can trust it.

Your data stays yours

Readings are analyzed on your device and stored only in your browser. No account required. Delete everything in one tap.

Minimum necessary

The AI coach receives summary statistics only — never raw readings, dates, names, or device IDs.

Clinical guardrails

No dosing advice, ever. Emergencies and crisis language trigger fixed safety responses that don't depend on the AI.

For clinics, health plans & employers

Better visits. Better outcomes. Lower cost.

Betawise gives patients a reason to look at their data between visits, and gives care teams a standardized summary the moment the visit starts. We partner with endocrinology and primary care practices, payers, and employers to bring guided CGM coaching to their populations.
