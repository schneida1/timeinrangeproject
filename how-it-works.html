The Mentorship Journey

A structured, data-driven, and deeply empathetic 6-month program designed to transform how you interact with your diabetes data and your daily life.

01

Phase 1: The Match

Weeks 1 - 2

We don't just pair you with the first available volunteer. We use a detailed intake process to match you with a mentor who mirrors your reality. If you are a 30-year-old with T1D using a Dexcom G7 and a Tandem Mobi pump, we pair you with someone who knows that exact hardware and the lifestyle challenges that come with it.

  • Comprehensive lifestyle and technology intake.
  • Introduction call to establish boundaries, goals, and communication styles.
02

Phase 2: The Baseline & AGP Review

Weeks 3 - 4

Before making any changes, we look at where you are right now—with zero judgment. You and your mentor will review your Ambulatory Glucose Profile (AGP) from the last 14 days. The AGP is the standardized report that condenses all your CGM data into one clear picture.

Together, you will identify your "lowest hanging fruit." Is it a massive spike after breakfast? Are you dropping low at 3:00 AM? We pick one single metric to focus on first so you don't feel overwhelmed.

03

Phase 3: Real-World Adjustments

Months 2 - 5

This is where the magic happens. Through weekly check-ins (via video call or text, depending on your preference), your mentor teaches you the "street smarts" of diabetes management that aren't covered in a 15-minute doctor's visit.

What We Cover:

  • Mastering the pre-bolus timing.
  • Navigating exercise without crashing.
  • Managing pizza, pasta, and high-fat meals.
  • Overcoming the mental block of "rage bolusing."

"My endocrinologist told me to take my insulin 15 minutes before eating. My mentor taught me that if I'm eating oatmeal, it needs to be 25 minutes, but if I'm having eggs, I can take it right when I eat. That distinction changed my life."

04

Phase 4: Graduation & Paying it Forward

Month 6

By month six, our goal is that you no longer need us. You will have a personalized playbook for your body, a higher Time in Range, and renewed confidence. At graduation, many of our mentees choose to complete our coaching certification to become mentors themselves, passing their newly refined knowledge onto the next person who needs it.
