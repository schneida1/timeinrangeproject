Our Mission

Living with Type 1 Diabetes is a constant balancing act. At The Time in Range Project, we believe that focusing on Time in Range—rather than just an A1C score—provides a more complete, empowering, and actionable picture of diabetes management.

Our goal is to ensure everyone with T1D has access to the Continuous Glucose Monitor (CGM) technology, mental health support, and education required to safely increase the hours they spend feeling their best.

Why "Time in Range"?

Time in Range (TIR) represents the percentage of time a person's blood sugar levels remain within a target range, typically defined as 70 to 180 mg/dL.

More Than Just A1C

While A1C provides an average of blood sugar over three months, it doesn't show the daily highs and lows. Two people can have the exact same A1C, but wildly different daily experiences. By focusing on maximizing TIR, individuals experience fewer dangerous extreme lows (hypoglycemia), fewer exhausting highs (hyperglycemia), and an overall better quality of life.
