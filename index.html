Why A1C Doesn't Tell the Whole Story

Two people can have an identical A1C of 7.0%. Person A stays steady between 70-180 mg/dL all day. Person B bounces dangerously between 40 mg/dL and 250 mg/dL. Person A feels great. Person B feels exhausted. That is why we focus strictly on Time in Range.

How Peer Mentorship Works

1

The Match

We pair you with a mentor who shares your diagnosis (T1D or T2D), uses similar management technology (pump, MDI, specific CGM), and understands your lifestyle.

2

The Baseline

Together, you'll review your current data without judgment. You will identify specific patterns—like post-meal spikes or overnight lows—and pick one target to fix first.

3

The Adjustment

Through weekly calls and text support, your mentor shares practical, lived-experience strategies for pre-bolusing, exercise adjustments, and mental burnout.
