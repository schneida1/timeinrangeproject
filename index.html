How Peer Mentorship Works

1

The Match

We pair you with a mentor who shares your diagnosis (T1D or T2D), uses similar management technology, and understands your lifestyle.

2

The Baseline

Together, you'll review your current data without judgment. You will identify specific patterns and pick one target to fix first.

3

The Adjustment

Through weekly calls and text support, your mentor shares practical strategies for pre-bolusing, exercise adjustments, and mental burnout.
