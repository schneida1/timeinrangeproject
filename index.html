Why Time In Range?

70 - 180 mg/dL

Time in Range (TIR) measures the percentage of your day spent between 70 and 180 mg/dL. An A1C test only gives you a 3-month average. TIR tells you the whole story of your daily highs, lows, and stability.

More time in range means more energy, fewer mood swings, and less long-term burnout.

The Missing Link: Coaching

Endocrinologists give you the ratios, but they don't live in your shoes. Managing T1D and T2D happens at the dinner table, at the gym, and at 3 AM.

Our peer mentors have been in the trenches. They understand insulin timing, the impact of stress, insulin resistance, and the mental toll of chronic management.

How Our Peer Mentorship Works

We pair you with someone who has the same type of diabetes, utilizes similar management tools, and has a proven track record of maintaining a healthy Time in Range.
